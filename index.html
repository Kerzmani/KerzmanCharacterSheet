Backstory

Creation has always been something I enjoyed. From my prized short-story Wyndward, to campaign and character building for my D&D group Ignis Voltum, to building websites. The process of taking an idea and making it tangible, interactive, and alive is the reward in itself. I always hoped to find myself to be part of a community or company that loved sharing creation and spreading inspiration to young creatives to never let their dream of being a creative wither away under the pressures of the dull, mundane world.
